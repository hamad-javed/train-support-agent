# Refunds and cancellations

## If the train operator cancels your train
If your train is cancelled by the operator, you are entitled to a full refund of the ticket price with no admin fee, whatever your fare type.

## If you no longer want to travel
- Anytime and Off-Peak tickets can be refunded before the departure time. A £10 admin fee is deducted from the refund.
- Advance tickets are non-refundable if you simply change your mind.
- Tickets cannot be refunded after the train has departed, except under Delay Repay or if the train was cancelled.

Refunds are paid back to the original payment card within 5 working days.
