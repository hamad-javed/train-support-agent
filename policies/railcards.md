# Railcards

A Railcard saves you money on most rail fares in Great Britain.

- 16-25 Railcard: one third off most fares, for people aged 16 to 25 and full-time students. Costs £35 a year.
- 26-30 Railcard: one third off most fares. Costs £35 a year.
- Two Together Railcard: one third off for two named adults travelling together. Costs £35 a year.
- Senior Railcard: one third off for people aged 60 and over. Costs £35 a year.

Railcard discounts must be added when you book. They cannot be added to an existing booking afterwards. If you forget to bring your Railcard when travelling, the ticket inspector may charge you the full fare.
