# Delay Repay (compensation for late trains)

If your train arrives at your destination late, you can claim compensation under Delay Repay. The amount depends on how late you arrived, and is a percentage of the price you paid for that ticket:

- Delayed 15 to 29 minutes: 25% of the ticket price
- Delayed 30 to 59 minutes: 50% of the ticket price
- Delayed 60 to 119 minutes: 100% of the ticket price
- Delayed 120 minutes or more: 100% of the ticket price

Delays under 15 minutes are not eligible for compensation.

Delay Repay applies to every fare type, including Advance tickets, which are otherwise non-refundable.

You must claim within 28 days of the date you travelled. Only one Delay Repay claim can be made per booking. Compensation is paid back to the original payment card within 5 working days.
